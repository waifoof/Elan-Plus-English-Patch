# Known issues (v1.0.1)

Everything listed here is cosmetic. None of it affects play.

## Cosmetic / by design

- **Typed names have a small gap between letters.** The game reserves a full Japanese character
  width for each letter of the player's name, so names typed on the Alphabet tab are spaced out. The
  default name "Nao" shows as **"Na o"** in the name field and on the "Is this OK?" screen, because
  it is drawn as a two-letter cell plus a one-letter cell.
- **Save/load slots show "( Mo)" with a space.** The game positions the weekday field itself.
- **The day-change card says "Wednes day" / "Time to the Final test".** This is the original
  game's own English artwork and was left as designed.
- **The "POINTS" column header in the results tables is stacked as "POI / NTS".** This is also the
  original game's English artwork, left as designed.
- **Training buttons.** The buttons read "Solo Training" / "Joint Training", but each is made of
  two separate pieces placed by the game: a small "Solo"/"Joint" cell and a shared "Training" cell.
  The spacing between the two words is set by the game's layout.
- **The final exam's title card says "MONTHLY EXAM" at the top and bottom**, as the Japanese card does.
- **Short forms in small menu slots.** Some slots are too small for the full names and use
  "Sample Study", "Bio Catch", "SL Gate Flight", "Tot Rank" and similar. The exam movies,
  briefings, race header and mails use the full names.
- **Ages on the Partner Selection panel read "18 yo"** (the slot holds two letters).
- **Staff roll names.** The 12 voice actors, the composer and the character designer are spelled
  as officially credited elsewhere. Most other staff names are given in their most common reading
  and may be wrong; corrections are welcome. A few company names (Arowana Planning Office, Light
  Link Music, Project Uzu, Granpapa Production, Beginners Label) have no known official English form.

## Not translated

- The kana grid on the name-entry screen. It is the Japanese input keyboard; use the Alphabet tab.

## Not tested

- **Real PlayStation hardware.** All testing was done in an emulator (PCSX-ReARMed). The disc's
  sector error-correction data and file system were verified, but please report how it runs on a
  console or in DuckStation.

## What has been tested

The game was played from the first boot to the end credits on both the male and the female route,
and every ending has been played: partner accepted (all 8 candidates, both routes), the top scorer
picks you, not chosen, rejected, and the four rare staff endings (Narusawa, Rei, Sakaki, Sarai).

## Reporting

Please report anything else, with a screenshot and roughly where it happened: text that overflows
its box, reads oddly or is still in Japanese, wrong speaker names, or any freeze. See
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
