# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Talk screen: the help shown while holding R2 (the labels around the mood panel) is now all English.
  It was a mix of English words and leftover Japanese pieces ("Rapport レベル (6 Stag)"); every label
  is redrawn and the colour key reads Normal / Anger / Sad / Happy, as on the panel.

## v1.0.1

- A last full polish pass over every line (952 text blocks touched): awkward line breaks and lone
  words, choices that didn't lead into their replies, questions missing their "?", stiff or literal
  wording, repeated words, and a few small mistranslations. Narusawa's ending speech reads better in
  all 40 ending scenes.
- Title menu: "EXTRAS" is no longer clipped to "XTRAS"; the "Partner Select" banner is bold and readable.
- Training buttons read "Solo Training" / "Joint Training"; Exam Info reads "Wk04, Saturday"; the
  profile line is spaced properly.
- Sharper, more readable location and facility labels; name tags in one style; leftover marks
  removed (before "Yes", after "Who will you mail?", around "HELP", after "Tomoe", above "Exam Info").
- Name entry: the Hiragana / Katakana / Alphabet tabs use one font and no longer touch their frames.
- Prologue: the last caption now fades out with the scene (no blue boxes); captions are brighter.
- Exam title cards: "MONTHLY EXAM" labels are larger and evenly placed; the final exam card no
  longer flashes Japanese fragments.
- Staff roll: the cast columns are spaced apart.

## v1.0

- Extras: the Voice Actor Interviews and Gallery screens are now English (headers, all 12
  cast/character panels with the voice actors' names, the four interview questions, the help bars).
- Title menu: EXTRAS and INTERVIEWS now match the other menu items.
- The tiny dots above "Exam Results" are gone, and the "Candidates" panel is clean.
- Save/load slots read "Name  Wk 01 (Mo) 001 Day"; memory-card messages tidied; the default name is "Nao".
- Every ending was play-tested: a candidate accepting you as partner (all 8, both routes), the top
  scorer picking you, not being chosen, and being rejected, plus the four rare staff endings
  (Narusawa, Rei, Sakaki, Sarai). Small wording fixes in Yoh's and Narusawa's endings.
- The ending staff roll is in English (all 39 pages redrawn and re-laid out to fit the longer names).
- Each character's story was read start to finish for consistency; about 85 lines were tightened.

## v0.9

- Every menu, prompt and button label was redrawn in a clean typeface (Inter SemiBold) at one size,
  instead of the stretched pixel font: room/facility buttons, location names, mail and meeting
  prompts, name-entry and gender screens, results labels, training headers, Extras/Interviews.
- The Joint / Solo training buttons now show "Start Training" in full ("Normal" is now "Solo",
  matching the "Solo Training" header).
- The Saturday meeting prompt now reads "Who will you talk to?" (the stray mark before it was a
  clipped "W": the game builds this prompt from two pieces, as the Japanese does).
- "HELP" next to the R2 icon is redrawn crisp.
- Names typed on the Alphabet tab use wider, bolder letters (the game's fixed character width still
  leaves a small gap between letters).
- Partner Selection (end of the game): the prompt reads "Partner? Choose one." (it showed
  "?artner ...se a destination"), the "Partner Select" banner is bold and readable, and ages read
  "18 yo" instead of showing the Japanese age counter. The map prompt is "Where? Choose one." (the
  two prompts share their second half, as in the Japanese).
- Facility list: "Nav Range" is now "Flight Rm" (the dialogue calls it the Flight Training Room);
  the stray bar after it, the clipped "High-G Rm", the mark after "Port" and the "Solo Training"
  tick are gone.
- "Exam Rank" banner: both words the same height with a gap.
- Small wording fixes (Riko, Yoh, the final-exam mail "Subject: All areas").
- Play-tested from the start to the end credits on both the male and female routes (the final exam
  is in week 24).

## v0.8

- The lowest attitude level on the mood gauge now reads "Hate" (it was still Japanese).
- The Joint / Normal training buttons now show "Start Training" in full (it was cut off).
- "Exam Rank" banner: gap between the words; the stray tick in the "Solo / Joint Training" headers is gone.
- All exam names in the race header are centred the same way.
- Small wording fix in one of Yoh's conversations.

## v0.7

- Fixed: playing as a woman, the game could freeze at choice menus in story events (for example
  Riko's event in week 2). The patch now updates the game's "if the player is female" jumps in every
  scene, and the build checks every jump in every scene against the original.
- A full clarity edit of all English: every story scene, conversation, mail and menu was re-read
  against the Japanese for clear, natural, easy-to-read lines (about 2,500 lines improved: awkward
  line breaks, vague "it/that", stiff wording, choices that didn't lead into their replies, and
  several mistranslations).
- Repeated lines (meeting speeches, ending speeches) now read the same everywhere.
- Full exam names in the exam race header and the exam-notice mails ("Sample Analysis",
  "Wildlife Capture"...).
- The "Exam Rank" / "Total Rank" banners now have a gap between the words; the marks above
  "Exam Results", before "Talk to them?" and after "Solo" are gone.
- The developers' test mail (week 6) is translated.

## v0.6

- The monthly exam title cards are now English (they are short movies; all nine were re-encoded).
- The "Group Meeting", "Total Rank" and "Exam Rank" banners were redrawn in a bold outlined style,

- Removed stray marks above "Exam Results", under "Weekly Results" and before "Talk to them?".
- A few line breaks evened out (Kairi, Riko, Amane, Kaname).

## v0.5

## v0.4

- Fixed: stray dots and bars around letters in all text boxes.

- Weekday, week, day and rank labels translated; "Help" label fixed.
